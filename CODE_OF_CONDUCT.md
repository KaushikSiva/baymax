# Community code of conduct

We want Baymax to be a rigorous, welcoming place for robotics, AI, healthcare,
and safety practitioners to work together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and curious.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome different experience levels and professional backgrounds.
- Protect privacy. Never share patient data, credentials, or identifying clinical
  information.
- Treat medical-safety concerns seriously and avoid unsupported clinical claims.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and
repeatedly disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Security issues must
use private vulnerability reporting rather than a public issue. Maintainers may
edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.
