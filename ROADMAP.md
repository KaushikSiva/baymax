# Roadmap

The roadmap is organized around evidence and safety gates, not feature count.

## Now — simulation foundation

- [x] Unitree G1 MuJoCo model and pinned locomotion policy
- [x] Two-room collision-aware patrol
- [x] Patient speech start/finish and five-second silence behavior
- [x] Critical-monitor and fall incident payloads
- [x] Dummy and configurable HTTP dispatch
- [x] Browser operations view and evidence bundle
- [x] Deterministic end-to-end validation

## Next — observable integrations

- [ ] LiveKit session and room lifecycle
- [ ] Deepgram streaming transcription with confidence and timestamps
- [ ] MOSS-governed tool-call policy and approval traces
- [ ] Medplum sandbox resource update adapter
- [ ] Doctor voice-call sandbox with explicit human confirmation
- [ ] Stedi sandbox eligibility and claims workflow
- [ ] Recorded demo and reproducible benchmark scenarios

## Later — research and deployment gates

- [ ] Multi-patient scheduling and interruption handling
- [ ] Perception confidence calibration and uncertainty-aware escalation
- [ ] Accessibility and multilingual patient interaction
- [ ] Hardware-in-the-loop Unitree validation
- [ ] Threat model, privacy review, clinical safety case, and red-team suite
- [ ] Prospective evaluation with qualified clinical partners

Have a concrete use case or integration proposal? Open a
[feature request](https://github.com/KaushikSiva/baymax/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
